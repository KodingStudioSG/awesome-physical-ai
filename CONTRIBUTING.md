# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping improve Awesome Physical AI.

This repository is a curated list, not an exhaustive company or learning-resource database. Inclusion should help someone understand the organizations, projects, research, and skills shaping Physical AI.

## Before submitting

- Search the repository and existing pull requests to make sure the item is not already listed.
- Make one focused suggestion per pull request whenever practical.
- Use the official company, project, laboratory, conference, or course URL.
- Do not submit referral, affiliate, tracking, or shortened URLs.
- Keep descriptions factual, concise, and written in English.
- Avoid marketing slogans, funding claims, valuations, or unverifiable superlatives.
- Add the item alphabetically within the most relevant category when the section is alphabetical.

## What belongs here

A company, project, or resource should have a direct and substantial connection to Physical AI, such as:

- Robotics hardware or robot intelligence.
- Humanoid or general-purpose robots.
- Autonomous vehicles, drones, vessels, or mobile machines.
- Industrial, warehouse, agricultural, construction, or field robotics.
- Embodied AI, robot learning, perception, planning, control, or navigation.
- Simulation, synthetic data, or infrastructure built specifically for robotics and autonomous systems.
- Research groups or open-source projects with significant Physical AI work.
- High-quality learning materials that teach transferable robotics, autonomy, controls, perception, simulation, or embodied-AI skills.

Generic AI software, ordinary SaaS, purely digital agents, general cloud infrastructure, and companies with only incidental robotics work are usually out of scope.

## Entry format

Use this format:

```markdown
- [Project Name](https://example.com/) - Short, objective description that starts with a capital letter and ends with a period.
```

Descriptions should explain why the item belongs on the list rather than repeat the company tagline.

## Company suggestions

For a company addition, please include in the pull request description:

- Official website.
- Proposed category.
- One or two sentences explaining its direct Physical AI work.
- A primary source that demonstrates the relevant product, research, or deployment.
- Careers page if the company is actively hiring. This is useful for verification but does not determine inclusion.

## Learning resource suggestions

For additions to [Learning Physical AI](learning/README.md), prefer resources that are substantial, current, and directly useful for building real robotics or autonomy skills.

Good candidates include:

- University course materials and open textbooks.
- Official documentation with a meaningful learning path.
- Maintained open-source robotics curricula.
- Practical resources for ROS 2, perception, state estimation, controls, motion planning, robot learning, simulation, and embodied AI.

Please include the target section and a short explanation of what the learner should gain from the resource.

Low-value SEO articles, affiliate courses, generic AI introductions, link farms, and tutorial collections without a clear Physical AI learning objective are unlikely to be accepted.

## Quality bar

The maintainers may decline an entry even when it is technically in scope. We prefer a smaller list of notable, active, well-documented organizations and high-signal resources over an indiscriminate directory.

Entries may be removed if a project becomes inactive, is acquired and discontinued, loses its Physical AI focus, becomes badly outdated, or no longer has enough public information to verify its value.

## Corrections and removals

Corrections to names, descriptions, URLs, company status, resource status, or categorization are welcome. Please include a reliable source when the change is not self-evident.

Thank you for contributing.
